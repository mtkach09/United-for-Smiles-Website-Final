Who we are

Making dental health accessible to every child

United for Smiles is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to make preventive dental care reachable for every family — from Florida to communities across the globe.

Why we started

Something as basic as a toothbrush and toothpaste can prevent years of pain, missed school, and long-term health problems.

We believe access to oral hygiene should never depend on income or zip code. By distributing kits and educating communities, we're closing the gap one family at a time.

We're building with intention — real partnerships, real impact, and responsible growth.

Our mission

Every child deserves a healthy smile

Our mission is to close the gap in access to basic oral health care through supplies, education, and community partnership.

Educate

We teach children and families why daily oral hygiene and preventive care matter — and how to build habits that last.

Provide

We distribute complete hygiene kits to underserved children and families in the communities we serve.

Prevent

We stop dental disease before it starts: less pain, fewer missed school days, and lower emergency care costs.

Equalize

We address inequality in access to care by reaching the communities that are overlooked most often.
